What is Formula 1?

Formula 1 is the highest class of international racing for single-seater racing cars. It is one of the most prestigious and popular sporting events in the world, featuring the best drivers and teams competing on iconic circuits across the globe.

The championship began in 1950 and has since evolved into a pinnacle of motorsport technology and human performance. Each season, teams design and build powerful cars that push the boundaries of engineering and aerodynamics.

F1 combines speed, strategy, and skill. Races take place on specially designed circuits, some temporary street courses, with 24 races held each season. The sport demands exceptional physical fitness, mental concentration, and technical expertise from drivers who battle for supremacy in the most competitive racing series on Earth.
